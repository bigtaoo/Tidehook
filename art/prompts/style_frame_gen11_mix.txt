Mixed-media illustration for an indie mobile game: layered cut-paper creatures laid on top of an antique nautical sea chart.
Background: the sea is an old hand-drawn map on aged cream paper, with fine engraved ripple lines, thin depth contour lines and faded blue-green watercolour washes that get deeper blue toward the right, a small faded compass rose. On the far left a narrow vertical old wooden pier drawn like a cartographer's ink drawing, weathered planks and wooden posts wrapped in hemp rope, with a small iron rope winch; a hemp rope runs right across the map to an iron hook.
Foreground: every creature and object is a cut-out of matte coloured construction paper, cut with scissors, slightly uneven edges, visible paper fibre, a few overlapping paper layers for fins and claws, each casting a small soft drop shadow onto the map, like paper pieces laid on the chart and photographed from straight above. Small, at real gameplay scale, with lots of open map between them: five plump paper fish in orange, coral and golden yellow; a small paper treasure chest with brass trim; a paper driftwood log. Four paper sea monsters crawling left toward the pier: a crab with two big claws (one caught on the hook), a turtle with a big shell, a shark with a tall fin, an eel. Creatures have plain small dot eyes, no eyebrows, no teeth, no smiles, no cartoon mascot faces.
Far right: a narrow cream parchment sidebar panel with a thin brass frame, a coin and a simple bar. No text, no letters, no numbers anywhere. WIDE LANDSCAPE, top-down, no horizon, no sky.
