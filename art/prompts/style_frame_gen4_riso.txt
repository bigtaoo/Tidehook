Risograph screen-print illustration for an indie mobile game, printed with only four flat spot-color inks on warm off-white paper: teal, deep navy, burnt orange and mustard yellow. No black outlines at all; shapes are defined only by flat ink color. Visible paper grain, slight ink misregistration (layers offset by a hair), halftone dots in a few shadow areas. Hand-made, imperfect, slightly wobbly edges. Absolutely no gradients, no glossy highlights, no 3D rendering, no airbrushed shading.
Characters are simple, graphic and a little odd, like a children's picture book by a printmaker: small dot eyes, no eyebrows, no teeth, no smiles, no cartoon mascot faces.
Subject: a WIDE LANDSCAPE top-down in-game screenshot of a fishing-defense game. No horizon, no sky.
- Far left: a narrow vertical wooden pier of orange planks, with a small squat rope winch on it. A thin rope runs right across the water to a mustard brass hook.
- The sea: three vertical bands, teal near the pier, a middle band of teal overprinted with navy, deep navy on the right. A few small hand-drawn wave ticks.
- Small objects at real gameplay scale with lots of open water: five plump simple fish in orange and mustard, a small chest, a driftwood log.
- Four sea monsters crawling left toward the pier, printed only in navy and teal, each a strong simple silhouette: a crab with two big claws (one caught on the hook), a turtle with a blocky shell, a shark with a tall fin, an eel. Tiny orange dot eyes.
- Far right: a narrow plain sidebar panel of navy ink with a mustard coin and a simple bar. No text, no letters, no numbers anywhere.
